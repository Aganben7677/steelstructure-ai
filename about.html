About Steelstructure.AI

Daily intelligence and practical knowledge for the global steel structure industry

Built for steel structure teams that need project signals, not just news

steelstructure.ai is a daily intelligence and knowledge platform for the global steel structure industry. It connects project updates, EPC activity, supply-chain resources, technical standards, fabrication knowledge, and field observations into a practical industry reference.

The platform is shaped by practical experience in steel structure fabrication, international EPC project support, tender review, material standards, bolting, welding, coating, fireproofing, logistics, and project execution.

Our mission is to help fabricators, estimators, engineers, sourcing teams, and market teams find useful signals faster: what happened, why it matters, and how it may relate to steel structure opportunities.

Supply-chain mappingMills, fabricators, ports, warehouses, logistics resources, EPC offices, and regional capacity references.
Engineering knowledgeMaterials, bolting, welding, coating, fireproofing, fabrication, inspection, erection, logistics, and lessons learned.
